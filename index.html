Repairs & equipment

Find the fault. Fix the fault.

When something stops working, a clear description gets the right parts on the truck. Pick the closest match below, or just tell Chris what you’re seeing and hearing at the equipment pad.

01

Pumps

Won’t prime, loud, tripping the breaker or not turning on at all.

02

Filters

High pressure, cloudy water after cleaning, cracked housings and worn parts.

03

Heaters

No ignition, error codes or a heater that runs but never warms the water.

04

Salt systems

Low-salt warnings, cells that stop producing and readings that don’t add up.

05

Automation

Control panels, schedules and equipment that won’t respond.

06

Plumbing

Leaks at the pad, failing valves and lines that need rework.

FNS works on Pentair, Jandy and Hayward equipment.
